Lo Mein Noodles

Nothing beats the comfort of a bowl of silky, savory noodles. This classic Lo Mein is designed for the home kitchen—using simple ingredients you can find at any local grocery store. It’s the perfect "gateway" recipe for anyone just starting their journey into stir-fry cooking.

Description

These lo mein noodles were created from a blend of multiple recipes. Add your favorite meat for a main dish or serve as a side. If serving with meat, cook the meat separately.

Sweet and savory flavors come together in this takeout-inspired noodle dish, ready in just 30 minutes. Home cooks love customizing these noodles with vegetables, chicken, shrimp, or beef.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 ounce) package spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 3 stalks celery, sliced
  • 2 large carrots, cut into large matchsticks
  • ½ sweet onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 green onions, sliced plus more for garnish

Beginner Tip

Stir-frying happens fast! Before you turn on the heat, make sure all your vegetables are chopped and your sauce is mixed in a small bowl. This prevents the noodles from sticking.

Steps

  1. Step 01 Gather all ingredients.
  2. Step 02 Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes; drain, then rinse with cold water to cool.
  3. Step 03 Meanwhile, whisk soy sauce, teriyaki sauce, honey, and ginger together in a small bowl; set aside.
  4. Step 04 While the spaghetti cooks, heat oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Cook and stir celery, carrots, onion, and green onions until slightly tender, 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Step 05 Add spaghetti and soy sauce mixture. Cook, stirring frequently, until heated through and evenly coated, about 2 to 3 minutes. Serve garnished with additional sliced green onions, if desired.
